Load your ingredients

Ingredient numbers depend on your character's Cooking skill, so they differ per character. In-game, open Add new recipe and step through each category (pick a category number, then press x to go back) so every SELECT INGREDIENT list scrolls past — like the transcript you'd copy from your client. Paste that whole chunk below and it sorts each list into the right category automatically — using the category numbers you typed when present, otherwise assuming the lists appear in category order (Basics first, Condiments last). Nothing is loaded until you paste your own, so it only ever shows ingredients your character has actually seen.

Cooking methods & tastes

Cooking methods are character-specific (they depend on your Cooking skill), so none are bundled — paste your in-game COOKING METHOD list into the box below and save. Tastes are a fixed list and come pre-filled.

How it works

This mirrors the in-game cooking menu. To make a recipe you open the cooking menu, choose Manage recipes → Add new recipe, then for every ingredient you pick a category number followed by the ingredient number. After ingredients you choose Done (option 11), then a cooking method, a primary taste, type a description, choose a presentation, and type a name.

The Commands to type box gives you those inputs in order. Because the menus are numbered and those numbers shift with your character's Cooking skill, load your own lists under My Ingredients and Methods & Tastes so the generated numbers match what you see.

Everything you save stays in this browser (localStorage). Bookmark or keep this file and your lists persist.
